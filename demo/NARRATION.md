# Narration — UK AI Radar, WebMCP Challenge

Read at an even pace, no sell. British, unhurried. Each line sits over the beat named
beside it; the exact seconds come from `demo/out/transcript.json` after a run.
Total target: 2:30–2:50, under the 3-minute cap.

| Beat | Line |
|---|---|
| Cold open, the full list | "Every UK government document that moved this month. Grants, tenders, consultations. One founder is supposed to read all of it." |
| The agent appears | "This page offers its own tools to whatever agent the founder brought with them." |
| Profile fills, turns blue | "It fills in who they are — from what it already knows, not from a form they typed. The page marks it as the agent's guess until the founder says yes." |
| Filters move, cards flash | "Now it works the list. Every call moves the page the founder is looking at. They are not reading a transcript of what happened somewhere else." |
| draft_brief appears | "A new tool appears the moment a shortlist exists. The page told the agent it had something new to offer." |
| The private sentence | "Here is the part a website cannot do on its own. The founder says something to their agent that the site never sees — we lost our pilot partner, we are not a university, six weeks of runway." |
| The list shrinks | "The suggestions change. The page holds the data; the agent holds the context; the founder decides." |
| Drop with a reason | "When they turn something down, the reason goes back to the agent. It will not offer it again." |
| The brief | "And the brief is written from what survived the founder's judgement, not the model's." |
| Close | "It read all of it. They made the calls. That is the trade WebMCP makes possible." |

## Recording notes

- Voice over the silent screen capture; no music.
- If a line runs long against its beat, cut words, not the beat.
- The one claim to keep exact: the agent never sees the source data before the founder does, and the founder's private context never reaches the site.
